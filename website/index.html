Haven

Media player & tracker

Keep track of the films and TV shows you love. Know what's next, see what's coming out, and play from your own media servers on your home network.

Download What it does

Your films and shows, in one place

Haven contains no films or TV shows. It gives you information about them and keeps track of what you watch, and it plays only from your own media servers on your local network. Free and open source, with no ads and no account to make: everything stays on your own devices.

★

Track what you watch

A Watchlist, your history, watched marks on every episode, and Continue Watching to pick up where you left off.

📅

Never miss an episode

A calendar of what's coming out from the shows on your Watchlist, and an Upcoming row on Home.

⟳

Trakt and Simkl

Link your accounts and Haven keeps your watched history and Watchlist in step, both ways.

▶

Your own media servers

Play from your own Jellyfin, Plex and Emby servers on your local network.

📺

Live TV and radio

Add your own TV provider's playlist or account, with favourites and groups.

◎

Ratings and where to watch

IMDb, TMDB and Rotten Tomatoes scores, and which streaming services have a title in your country.

☁

Backups

Your servers, accounts, lists and settings are backed up, to iCloud Drive on iPhone and to your device elsewhere.

⊘

No films or shows inside

Haven doesn't host, sell or provide any films, shows or channels. It shows information and tracks what you watch.

⇪

Cast to your TV

Send what's playing to a Chromecast on your network.
