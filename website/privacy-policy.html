Haven Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Haven is a media player and tracker for films and TV shows, for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Tinus Christian Creeper ("we", "us"). Haven contains no films or TV shows: it shows information about them, keeps track of what you watch, and plays only from your own media servers on your local network. This policy explains what information Haven uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Haven app for iPhone, iPad and Android; where something works differently on one of them, it says so.

In short: Haven has no accounts with us and no ads, and it doesn't collect analytics or track you. We run no server for Haven and receive none of your information. What you track, your servers and your settings stay on your own devices, and your media servers are reached over your own local network. Haven only connects to the services that give it film and TV information and to the servers and accounts you add yourself.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • What you track: your Watchlist, what you watched and how far, Continue Watching, and the calendar of what's coming out.
  • Your servers and accounts: the addresses of the media servers you sign in to on your local network and the sign-in tokens they give Haven, your TV playlists and accounts, and your Trakt and Simkl sign-ins. Haven never keeps the passwords you type: they are sent once to your own server to sign in.
  • Settings and keys: your settings, and any API keys you enter in Settings › Keys.
  • Pictures and a log: posters and pictures are kept on your device so they show quickly. Haven also keeps a small log on your device to help find problems; it is never sent anywhere.
  • Videos on your device (optional): Library › Local lists videos already on your device. On Android it asks for permission to see your videos first. The list is only shown in Haven and is never sent anywhere.

2. Services Haven connects to

Haven connects to these services. They receive what's needed to answer, such as the title being looked up or the words you search for, along with your device's internet address, as with any website.

  • Film and TV information: TMDB (themoviedb.org), Cinemeta and OMDb (omdbapi.com), for catalogues, details, ratings and pictures. TMDB also tells Haven which streaming services have a title in your country, using the country you choose.
  • Your own media servers and TV providers: the media servers on your local network and the playlists and TV accounts you add. Haven tells your media server how far you watched, as its own apps do. Signing in to Plex goes through plex.tv.
  • Trakt and Simkl (optional): if you link an account, Haven sends it what you watch and your Watchlist changes, and brings in your history and Watchlist. What those services do with it is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • Player apps and casting (optional): if you choose another player app, or cast to a Chromecast on your network, the address of the video is handed to it.

We don't control these services and aren't responsible for them; their own privacy policies apply.

3. Backups

  • iPhone and iPad: Haven backs up your servers, accounts, lists and settings to your own iCloud Drive. Apple stores it in your iCloud account.
  • Android: the backup is saved in the Documents folder on your phone. Android's own backup may also keep Haven's data in your Google account, if you have it turned on.

Backups contain your sign-in tokens, so keep them private. We can't see or access them.

4. Children

Haven is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

5. Your choices

  • Remove a server, playlist or account in Settings at any time; Haven forgets its sign-in.
  • Unlink Trakt or Simkl in Settings › Accounts, or switch their sync off in Settings › Sync options.
  • Clear the kept pictures in Settings › Downloads & storage.
  • Deleting Haven deletes everything it keeps on that device. Backups you made stay where they were saved until you delete them.

6. How long we keep information

We don't keep any information about you: everything stays on your devices for as long as you keep it there.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when Haven changes. We'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
